## The deposit

```solidity
function deposit(uint256 units, uint8 ownerScheme, bytes32 owner) returns (uint64 nonce)
function depositAtLeast(uint256 units, uint8 ownerScheme, bytes32 owner, uint256 minMinted) returns (uint64 nonce)
```

The two are the same deposit. `depositAtLeast` also reverts, taking nothing, unless at least `minMinted` is minted:
the fee can rise with deposits that land just before, and this bounds what the depositor accepts. The web app and
the wallet always use it.

The vault:

1. requires `ownerScheme ≤ 4` and `owner ≠ 0`, and `minDeposit ≤ units ≤ maxDeposit`;
2. computes the fee and requires `minted = units − fee > 0` (and `minted ≥ minMinted`). The fee is
   `max(minDepositFee, ceil(units × feeBps / 10⁴))` while the whole deposit fits under half of the vault's deposit meter, and more for the part above it
   (the meter counts the deposits of the last 24 hours), up to `surgeFeeBps` (see [the rising deposit fee](/protocol/fees/#the-rising-deposit-fee));
3. pulls `units × scale` of the token and requires that its balance grew by exactly that amount (this refuses
   tokens that charge a transfer fee);
4. assigns the next nonce: deposits are numbered 0, 1, 2, … per vault;
5. adds the fee to `feesAccrued` and records `depositMinted[nonce] = minted` for a possible refund;
6. emits the event:

```solidity
event Deposit(uint64 indexed nonce, address indexed sender, uint8 ownerScheme,
              bytes32 owner, uint256 units, uint256 fee, uint256 minted);
```

**Every nonce is minted on Kaspa exactly once, or refunded.** The minted amount is the event's `minted` field,
never more.

## The mint

`BridgeMint.mint` mints 1 to 3 consecutive deposits of one chain, starting exactly at that chain's next nonce.
The covenant checks, on-chain, whoever builds the transaction:

1. Minting is not paused. Every recipient has `amount > 0`, a valid owner scheme, borrow scheme `0x00`, borrow
   guard of 32 zero bytes, and extension commitment of 32 zero bytes.
2. `first_nonce` equals the chain's next nonce. The nonce advances by the number of recipients.
3. `age_daa` is at most the age of the minter UTXO. The input's sequence is a relative lock: consensus accepts the
   transaction only once the UTXO is that old, which proves the age without trusting the submitter. The spend starts
   a new UTXO at age zero, so a stretch of time can refill the allowance only once.
4. The total is at most the refilled allowance and at most `remaining` (the hard cap).
5. Three signatures by distinct signers, in increasing signer order, over the **mint digest**.
6. The successor BridgeMint carries exactly the updated state and the same KAS value, and each minted output is
   a standard `KCC20` state equal to its recipient.

```text
mint digest = BLAKE3( "kUSDT/mint/v2" ‖ covenant_id[32] ‖ chain[1] ‖ first_nonce[8] ‖ age_daa[8]
                      ‖ for each recipient: owner_scheme[1] ‖ owner[32] ‖ amount[8] )
```

All integers are 8-byte little-endian. The digest binds the deployment, the chain, the nonces, every recipient
and every amount, so a signature cannot be reused for anything else.

## What signers check before signing

The specification requires every signer to check every request against its own nodes, and to take nothing the
coordinator says as fact. For a mint, that means a signer signs only if:

- the deposit's block is final and every one of its EVM RPCs reports the same event, field for field (see [Finality](/protocol/finality/));
- the recipients and amounts in the digest equal the events' `ownerScheme`, `owner` and `minted`, nonce by nonce;
- the vault's `depositMinted(nonce)`, read on every RPC, is not zero, so a refunded nonce is never minted;
- it has not signed a different mint starting at that nonce.

The signer does not read the next nonce from Kaspa: BridgeMint itself refuses a mint that does not start at that chain's next nonce.

## Ordering and batching

| Rule | Effect |
|---|---|
| One chain per mint | A mint carries `chain = 0x01` or `0x02`, never both |
| Consecutive nonces | A batch of up to 3 deposits must be `n, n+1, n+2` |
| Separate counters | `nonce_ethereum` and `nonce_bsc` advance independently |
| No skipping | A deposit cannot be passed over except by `skip_nonce` (4 of 5), followed by a refund |

Because mints follow the nonces strictly, a deposit that can never be minted would block every later deposit
of its chain. `skip_nonce` exists for that case; see [Refunds](/protocol/refunds/).

## Who pays for the minted outputs

The minter keeps its own KAS deposit (`minter.value == self.value`). The covenant leaves the KAS of the new
token outputs to whoever submits the mint. In kUSDT, the coordinator's Kaspa funding pays it, together with the
network fee, out of the deposit fee. Each minted state carries 1.5 KAS, which belongs to the holder from then on
and travels with the token. See
[Storage mass](/kaspa/storage-mass/).
