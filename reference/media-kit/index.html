Brand and media kit

For anyone who writes about kUSDT, lists it or integrates with Trestly. Every file here is one the project uses itself, unchanged. For questions, ask in t.me/trestly.

Names

Section titled “Names”
Write For
Trestly The product: the bridge, its web app and these docs
kUSDT The token on Kaspa: a lower-case k, then USDT in capitals
kUSDT Wallet The browser extension
KasPool The KAS⇄kUSDT pool on Kaspa L1

kUSDT is not USDT and is not issued by Tether (FAQ). Don’t write “USDT” when you mean kUSDT, and don’t shorten kUSDT to anything else.

Not in the kit

Section titled “Not in the kit”
  • No wordmark file. The web app writes the word “Trestly” as text beside the mark, in Figtree at weight 600 (see Typefaces). There is no file of the word, or of the mark and the word together.
  • No one-colour version. There is no white, black or single-colour version of the mark.
  • No kUSDT logo of its own. The web app and kUSDT Wallet show kUSDT as Tether’s mark with a small Kaspa badge. Those marks belong to their owners, so that picture is not in this kit.

Typefaces

Section titled “Typefaces”
Use Typeface
All text, and the word Trestly beside the mark Figtree, the variable font. The word Trestly is set at weight 600, letter spacing −0.012 em. Figtree is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
Addresses, IDs and code The system’s monospace font (ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, 'Liberation Mono', monospace)

Where Figtree is missing, the web app falls back to the system’s sans-serif font.

Do’s and don’ts

Section titled “Do’s and don’ts”

Do:

  • use the files as they are, at their own proportions;
  • write the names as in Names;
  • link to the web app or to these docs for anything about how kUSDT works.

Don’t:

  • change the logos: no new colours, no redrawing, stretching, rotating, cropping or effects, no words added to them;
  • imply a partnership, an endorsement or a review by Trestly that does not exist;
  • use the name or the marks for another token, a fork, or a copy of the bridge, the web app or the wallet;
  • call kUSDT USDT, or say that Tether issues it.

Other projects’ marks

Section titled “Other projects’ marks”

The Ethereum, BNB Chain, Kaspa and USDT logos belong to their owners and are not part of this kit. The web app shows them to name the networks and the tokens. Take them from their owners, on their terms.

Using the name

Section titled “Using the name”
  • You may say that you support kUSDT, or that your product integrates with Trestly.
  • You may not present yourself as Trestly or as its operators, or speak for them.
  • Point your users to the project’s own channels: the web app (trestly.io), these docs, t.me/trestly and x.com/trestly_io.

For an integration, see Integrate with Trestly and Risks to tell your users.
